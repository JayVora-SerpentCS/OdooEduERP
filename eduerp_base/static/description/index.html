School Management

School Management helps you streamline academic and student operations with ease. From configuring classes, subjects, and academic years to managing admissions, student profiles, and grading systems — everything is accessible in one place. Stay updated with reminders, news, and dashboards while saving time with consolidated student information such as personal details, family info, awards, and certificates.
